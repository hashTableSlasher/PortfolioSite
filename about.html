About

Over the course of my education (both inside and outside of school), I've worked to hone my creative and technical skills to accommodate the skills I'd need to meet the web. The web is a weird place, it's the world's biggest group project and it reflects that. For good or bad, the web reflects everyone; It is the a case study of human behavior, self expression and technical innovation. I sit in the camp of people that believe that the subjects that we were taught in school were never really separate things. Engineering, math, history, human behavior, art and science are all parts of the same whole that inevitably bleed into each other. The web reflects thats bleeding bit and that's why I'm so excited to set up camp as a web developer.
